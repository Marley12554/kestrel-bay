# Fifth Avenue for Unreal

The native desktop project opens at the Fifth Avenue sidewalk near East 33rd Street. Press **J** to visit 1350 Broadway at Herald Square and press it again to return to your previous position and view. The additional production block lies immediately north, between Broadway, Sixth Avenue, West 36th and West 37th Streets. The surrounding Midtown survey supplies the existing city context.

WASD moves or drives. Space jumps, Shift sprints or applies the handbrake, E enters or exits the nearby sedan, operates a swing door or orders coffee at the Reserve counter. Push into a revolving leaf to turn it. The arrow keys toggle the turn signals. T switches between day and night. R reloads the prototype at the starting sidewalk. F leaves the standing player in place and enters freecam. E and Q change flight height, Escape adjusts flight speed, and F returns to the original view. H toggles the HUD, B cycles full, reduced and disabled camera motion, and V releases the cursor until the next click. Escape opens the graphics and mouse sensitivity sliders. A fresh user profile starts on Low and subsequent launches load the saved choice. Mouse sensitivity applies to walking and driving. At 100%, a horizontal or vertical movement of 100 raw mouse counts rotates the view 3.5°. The slider offers finer adjustment at the slow end and saves on release.

## Build and measure the desktop game

Ordinary play uses a 60 FPS cap. The benchmark runs uncapped. Launch the packaged executable with `-AvenueRoadTest` to exercise the Herald Square round trip and drive across the painted intersection. It records storefront, moon and road screenshots in the game’s Saved directory. In a Development build, use `-AvenueInputTest` with a separate `-UserDir` to check first-frame walking and reversal, stopping, simultaneous turn and strafe, pitch direction, and dragging the actual settings slider outside its track. It also prints frame times for two full turns on the selected graphics preset. Use `-AvenueBlockTest` to walk through the Reserve entrances, order and collect a coffee, exercise freecam, drive over the surveyed curb, hold the two Teslas in contact, and reset with R.

The 3D renderer targets a 16.67 ms GPU frame budget. Low can vary from 60% to 72% internal resolution and Ultra from 75% to 100%. Platforms without dynamic resolution use the preset's upper limit. Menus render at the viewport resolution.

The native assets and level are checked in. On Mac, run `scripts/unreal/package-mac.sh` from the checkout with Unreal 5.8, Xcode and its Metal shader compiler installed. The script produces `dist/unreal-mac/Mac/FifthAvenue.app`. On Windows, run `scripts\unreal\package-windows.cmd` with Unreal 5.8, the Visual Studio C++ game development tools and the Windows SDK installed. It cooks the same map and stages the game and its prerequisites under `dist\unreal-windows`. Both scripts accept `UE_ROOT` for a nonstandard engine location.

Launch the packaged executable with `-AvenueBench -windowed -ResX=1512 -ResY=982 -ForceRes -NoVSync`. The benchmark allows six seconds for startup, measures the same twenty-second street camera route twice, and exits. The second pass adds eight collision traces and short tracer effects per burst, plus four pooled local flashes. Results include average, 95th, 99th and worst wall-clock frame times, and the number exceeding 16.67 ms. This is a synthetic effects load. It does not measure a playable combat AI or networked match. Add `-AvenueBlockBench` to run the route on the new West 36th Street block, or add `-AvenueDriveBench` to exercise the sedan, including acceleration, braking and steering. The game writes `AvenueBench.csv` in its Saved directory and prints the same measurements in its log. An optional `-AvenueCapture` saves a screenshot before the timing window.

## Survey and asset provenance

The existing city geometry, commercial signs, vehicles, characters, recorded gaits, sound and lunar image come from the Fifth Avenue preview. See the source credits under `preview/winter`, `preview/audio`, and `preview/people`. Unreal uses centimeters. Each Godot point (east, height, south) maps to Unreal (east, south, height) multiplied by 100. The origin, CRS and vertical datum are defined in `scripts/survey/frame.json`.

The adjacent block uses NYC CityGML buildings 1015241–1015244 on tax block 812. The LiDAR production area extends the existing pilot north using the same NYC elevation raster and sidewalk survey. The façades retain the surveyed massing and setbacks. The Greenwich Savings Bank's Broadway portico, West 36th Street engaged columns, street-level openings and attic follow the NYC Landmarks Preservation Commission's [exterior designation](https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/lpc/lp/1766.pdf). Office bay dimensions and material finishes on the surrounding parcels are estimates. The low-rise contains Selvedge, an original fabric shop with a recessed shop floor and counters. The bank recreation covers its exterior. The Commission also publishes the [interior designation](https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/lpc/lp/1767.pdf).

## Rebuild the source assets

1. Prepare the CityGML cache using the existing `scripts/survey` workflow. Bake the extended grade with `scripts/survey/grade.py DEM.tif --core 301008 64816 301208 65016 --out dist/unreal-staging/grade.json` and run `scripts/unreal/west36.py` in the same Python environment.
2. Set `FIFTH_STAGING` to the absolute `dist/unreal-staging` directory. Run Godot in a window with an active renderer. The dummy headless renderer discards instance poses. Use `--path preview --rendering-method gl_compatibility --script /absolute/path/to/scripts/unreal/export_street.gd` to read the instance transforms and colors. Run `export_people.gd` in the same project, then `python3 scripts/unreal/join_people.py "$FIFTH_STAGING"`. Convert the original Ogg footstep recordings to WAV in that directory, retaining their filenames. Run `python3 scripts/unreal/winter_audio.py "$FIFTH_STAGING"` to create the seamless airflow, winter street and indoor room recordings. The street export also supplies the snow field and road mask used by footsteps, tire tracks and the ground material.
3. Run `python3 scripts/unreal/sky_panorama.py` with NumPy and FFmpeg installed to convert the original street panorama. Compile the FifthAvenueEditor target. With `FIFTH_ROOT` set to the checkout, run the Unreal Python commandlet successively on `import_assets.py`, `materials.py`, `build_level.py` and `validate_level.py`. The checked-in level can be packaged without regenerating these assets.

## Desktop verification scope

The repaired Mac package ran in a 1512 × 982 window on the 14-inch M1 Pro using Low (72% internal resolution). The uncapped samples below cover twenty seconds each. A frame above 16.67 ms misses the 60 FPS target.

| Route | Mean (ms) | 99th percentile (ms) | Worst (ms) | Frames above 16.67 ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fifth Avenue driving | 8.40 | 10.05 | 74.19 | 5 |
| Fifth Avenue driving with effects | 8.38 | 9.46 | 71.18 | 3 |
| West 36th Street | 8.43 | 12.07 | 70.24 | 8 |
| West 36th Street with effects | 8.39 | 9.78 | 63.25 | 3 |

In a separate instrumented drive, a 90.6 ms frame used 7.2 ms of GPU time. The game thread spent 78.4 ms waiting and the render thread spent 48.3 ms waiting on visibility work. The profiler recorded zero garbage collections in that frame and zero asynchronous asset load requests over the capture. Moving visibility work to the render thread shifted the stalls and failed to improve the worst case across both passes. The game uses Unreal’s default scheduling. The 60 FPS minimum remains unmet. Input-to-display latency, fan speed and sustained combat performance still need hardware measurements.

The installed game returned the walker to the starting position and view after two J presses, then drove 279 feet on Low and 281 feet on Ultra across the painted intersection. On both presets, pressing a movement key or reversing direction moved the character within the first game frame. Releasing the key stopped the character. The settings slider reached and saved exactly 100% sensitivity. Over two complete turns, the 99th-percentile frame took 10.60 ms on Low and 17.57 ms on Ultra.

The café route went through the revolving entrance in both directions, opened the swing door, ordered coffee and collected the offered cup. The car turned its front wheels while parked, climbed a six-inch curb at 19.8 mph, threw powder from the snowy patch and kept the driver's feet inside the cabin. During 218 frames of sustained Tesla-to-Tesla contact in normal capped play, the 99th percentile was 16.90 ms and the slowest frame was 70.59 ms. R returned the scene to its initial state. The level check traced the ground at 289 positions around Herald Square. In-game captures cover the Reserve interior, sculpture, handoff, sunlight, street lighting and textured moon.

The pilot includes the existing Midtown survey and one additional detailed block. The ordinary fabric shop is fictional. The landmark bank has an exterior recreation. The two traffic cars follow the signals, pedestrians use the walking interval, and tires and footsteps leave impressions in the Fifth Avenue snow field. Windows packaging, Windows hardware performance, and automatic delivery of desktop builds have not been verified.
