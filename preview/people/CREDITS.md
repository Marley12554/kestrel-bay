The player, first-person hand, barista and pedestrians use Innerscene’s CC0 adaptations of MakeHuman and MPFB characters. The game resizes the source textures, selects locomotion clips and uses a posed section of Sam’s mesh for the first-person coffee grip.

- [Sam](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/locomotion-collection-sam-3d-person-animated-dc23800f)
- [Maya](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/locomotion-collection-maya-3d-person-animated-1499b2fd)
- [Jordan walking](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/walk-carrying-briefcase-jordan-3d-person-animated-c805c97a) and [standing](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/idle-standing-jordan-3d-person-animated-1721c58f)
- [Harper walking](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/relaxed-walk-harper-3d-person-animated-b504cbac) and [standing](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/idle-standing-harper-3d-person-animated-ba9bc23f)

The yellow cab uses tevy’s CC0 [Yellow Taxi Cab](https://tevy.dev/models/yellow-taxi), version 1. Its mesh compression was decoded for Godot.

Street walking uses normal, slow and brisk strides from [Carnegie Mellon University's Motion Capture Database, subject 07, trials 01, 04 and 12](https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/search.php?subjectnumber=7). The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217. The marker sampling and rig retargeting scripts are in `scripts/people`.
