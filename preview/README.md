# Midtown street preview

Start on foot on Fifth Avenue between 33rd and 34th streets. Walk with WASD at 4.8 mph. Press Space to jump. Hold Shift to sprint at 16 mph, or tap Sprint on touchscreens to toggle it. Press **F** to leave your body and fly, and **F** again to return. In freecam, use the slider for 2–2,400 mph, E to climb and Q to descend. Click the street once for mouse look with a centered crosshair. V releases the pointer. Escape pauses the game and opens a graphics slider. Performance, Balanced and High adjust rendering resolution, crowd distance, snow detail and lighting. The game remembers the setting on this device. Windows and Web start on Performance. On touchscreens, drag to look. Press E by the sedan to drive, then press E while stopped to get out. H hides or restores the HUD. Footsteps follow distance traveled and blend with the snow underfoot. B cycles full, low, and off for camera bob, sprint field of view, and peripheral blur. The web footer has the same control and defaults to off when the device requests reduced motion.

The east-side corner reconstructs the historic Demarest Building on its surveyed footprint. [Beyond My Ken’s corner photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:339_Fifth_Avenue.jpg) shows the two Fifth Avenue arches, numbered entrance and shops. [Tdorante10’s 33rd Street photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:33rd_St_5th_Av_04_-_Demarest_Building.jpg) shows the three side arches, paired attic windows and narrow end bay. The modeled glass, masonry and metalwork use these images as references. The closed vestibule, fixtures behind the glazing and winter lighting are interpretations. The Fifth Avenue entrance, West 34th Street observatory entrance and holiday storefronts are modeled for this block. The [Fifth Avenue lobby reference](https://www.twotwelve.com/project/empire-state-building) and [visitor entrance directions](https://www.esbnyc.com/hours-directions) informed the layout.

Geometry comes from the [Technical University of Munich NYC CityGML project](https://www.asg.ed.tum.de/gis/projekte/new-york-city-3d/) and the underlying NYC Open Data building and street surveys. Building shapes reflect the 2014 survey. The source places streets on a flat plane. The legacy preview aligns building bases with that plane. The Herald Square pilot uses the surveyed terrain and a common vertical datum.

The committed compressed Godot scene builds without the source ZIPs. To regenerate it, put the citywide archives in `source-assets/NYC-CityGML/`, run `python3 scripts/extract-midtown.py`, then run `python3 scripts/mesh-midtown.py` with NumPy, Shapely 2.1+ and mapbox-earcut installed. Finish with `godot --headless --path preview --script ../scripts/pack-midtown.gd`. Use the Web export preset in the preview project for deployment. Regenerate the walking and driving collision resource with `python3 scripts/midtown-street-collision.py` and `godot --headless --path preview --script ../scripts/pack-midtown-walls.gd`.

## Herald Square survey pilot

Press **T** to switch between evening and daylight in place, including while driving or using freecam. The web footer also has a Day / night button. Press **J** on foot to visit 1350 Broadway. Press **J** again to return to the same spot on Fifth Avenue. The block fills the wedge between Broadway, Sixth Avenue, West 35th and West 36th. Walk up Broadway to the bronze 1350 entrance, then around to the freight entrance on West 36th. The street surfaces, pedestrian ground contact and four tire contacts follow the survey. [Pilot sources, controls and bake instructions](../scripts/survey/README.md)

## Native winter block

The browser build opens the winter block using Compatibility. The macOS build starts in the evening on Fifth Avenue and uses Metal with Forward+ lighting. Approach the sedan and press E to drive. Press E at rest to exit. W and S brake before selecting the opposite direction after a short hold at rest, and A/D steer. Tap the left/right arrows to signal. The same key cancels, as does straightening after a turn. Look or drag to orbit. The single-speed electric drivetrain, tire wash, wheel rotation and body pitch follow the car’s motion. The Mac build captures the mouse at startup. Press V to release it and click the street to capture it again. F toggles freecam on foot. H hides the control hint. The downloadable art credits are in [winter/CREDITS.txt](winter/CREDITS.txt).

With the Godot 4.7.1 macOS release template installed, run `godot --headless --path preview --editor --import --quit`, then `godot --headless --path preview --export-release "Mac Native" "../dist/native/Fifth Avenue.app"` after creating the destination directory.

The Fifth Avenue base uses [this dated entrance and retail photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EmpireStateBuilding20205thAvenueEntrance.jpg) for its proportions and historic shop signs and [the entrance manufacturer's photographs](https://www.dawsonmetal.com/architectural-entrances/products/historical-renovations) for the door metalwork. The 33rd Street return and display arrangements are interpretations. Fitted shop interiors are deferred.

The Starbucks Reserve ground floor occupies the Empire State Building corner at Fifth and 34th. Walk into a revolving wing to push through at either entrance. Use E at the glass swing door, at the barista to order, and again to collect the finished cup. The retail entrance, bronze display surrounds, terrazzo, merchandise islands and dark stone counter follow [the project designer's photographs](https://www.johnnelsondesign.com/empire-state-building) and [Starbucks’ Level 1 guide](https://www.starbucksreserve.com/locations/esb/explorebylevel). The interior dimensions and service circulation are estimated from those views. The colorful installation and wall artwork are original interpretations. This pass covers Level 1.

### Revolving doors in generated storefronts

Fit each opening to the ends of the fixed curved glass. Recess the full sweep of the wings behind the street face, join the glass returns to the storefront jambs, and seat the threshold on the finished floor. Check the installed entrance at eye height from the street, at an angle, and from inside. Exercise the player capsule through a rotating compartment in both directions. The Reserve regression in `tests/midtown-block.gd` covers the sweep and passage separately from the adjacent swing door.
