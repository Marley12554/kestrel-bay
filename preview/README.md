# Midtown street preview

Start on foot on Fifth Avenue between 33rd and 34th streets. Walk with WASD at 4.8 mph. Hold Shift to sprint at 16 mph, or tap Sprint on touchscreens to toggle it. Press **F** to leave your body and fly, and **F** again to return. In freecam, use the slider for 2–2,400 mph, E to climb and Q to descend. Click the street once for mouse look with a centered crosshair. V or Escape releases the pointer. On touchscreens, drag to look. Press E by the teal sedan to drive, then E while stopped to get out. H hides or restores the HUD. Footsteps follow distance traveled and blend with the snow underfoot. B cycles full, low, and off for camera bob, sprint field of view, and peripheral blur. The web footer has the same control and defaults to off when the device requests reduced motion.

The east-side corner uses the layered arched bays of the historic [Demarest Building at 339 Fifth](https://6tocelebrate.org/site/empire-state-building-339-fifth-avenue/) as a reference. Doorways and storefronts are closed exteriors. The Fifth Avenue entrance, West 34th Street observatory entrance and holiday storefronts are modeled for this block. The [Fifth Avenue lobby reference](https://www.twotwelve.com/project/empire-state-building) and [visitor entrance directions](https://www.esbnyc.com/hours-directions) informed the layout.

Geometry comes from the [Technical University of Munich NYC CityGML project](https://www.asg.ed.tum.de/gis/projekte/new-york-city-3d/) and the underlying NYC Open Data building and street surveys. Building shapes reflect the 2014 survey. The source places streets on a flat plane. Building bases are aligned with that plane while preserving individual building heights. The rendering uses illustrative surface colors.

The committed compressed Godot scene builds without the source ZIPs. To regenerate it, put the citywide archives in `source-assets/NYC-CityGML/`, run `python3 scripts/extract-midtown.py`, then run `python3 scripts/mesh-midtown.py` with numpy and mapbox-earcut installed. Finish with `godot --headless --path preview --script ../scripts/pack-midtown.gd`. Use the Web export preset in the preview project for deployment. Regenerate the walking and driving collision resource with `python3 scripts/midtown-street-collision.py` and `godot --headless --path preview --script ../scripts/pack-midtown-walls.gd`.

## Native winter block

The browser build opens the winter block using Compatibility. The macOS build starts in the evening on Fifth Avenue and uses Metal with Forward+ lighting. Approach the sedan and press E to drive. W accelerates, S brakes and then selects reverse if held at rest, and A/D steer. Look or drag to orbit. The single-speed electric drivetrain, tire wash, wheel rotation and body pitch follow the car’s motion. The Mac build captures the mouse at startup. Press V to release it and click the street to capture it again. F toggles freecam on foot. H hides the control hint. The downloadable art credits are in [winter/CREDITS.txt](winter/CREDITS.txt).

With the Godot 4.7.1 macOS release template installed, run `godot --headless --path preview --editor --import --quit`, then `godot --headless --path preview --export-release "Mac Native" "../dist/native/Fifth Avenue.app"` after creating the destination directory.

The Fifth Avenue base uses [this dated entrance and retail photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EmpireStateBuilding20205thAvenueEntrance.jpg) for its proportions and historic shop signs and [the entrance manufacturer's photographs](https://www.dawsonmetal.com/architectural-entrances/products/historical-renovations) for the door metalwork. The 33rd Street return and display arrangements are interpretations. Fitted shop interiors are deferred.
