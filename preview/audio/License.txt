Vehicle audio

The indicator on/off samples are edited from NHumphrey's interior recording
"Vehicle's Turning Signal - On.wav" (CC0).
https://freesound.org/people/NHumphrey/sounds/200976/

The rolling and sustained tire scuff layers use filtered excerpts from
bmacphail's highway and wet pavement recording (CC0).
https://freesound.org/people/bmacphail/sounds/795866/
The short breakaway chirp is edited from RutgerMuller's "Tires Squeaking.aif" (CC0).
https://freesound.org/people/RutgerMuller/sounds/104026/

The Model 3 pedestrian warnings are trimmed and crossfaded excerpts from
Tesla's official Drive and Reverse examples. Source recordings by Tesla.
https://service.tesla.com/docs/Public/diy/model3/en_us/GUID-AA58ED67-9C93-4EE6-8B19-9FDABE018787.html
Drive: https://service.tesla.com/docs/Public/om_media/pws_drive_forward.mp3
Reverse: https://service.tesla.com/docs/Public/om_media/pedestrian_speaker.mp3
