Vehicle audio

The indicator on/off samples are edited from NHumphrey's interior recording
"Vehicle's Turning Signal - On.wav" (CC0).
https://freesound.org/people/NHumphrey/sounds/200976/

The rolling layer is a filtered, looped constant-speed excerpt from
bmacphail's highway and wet pavement recording (CC0).
https://freesound.org/people/bmacphail/sounds/795866/
The skid loop is edited from RutgerMuller's "Tires Squeaking.aif" (CC0).
https://freesound.org/people/RutgerMuller/sounds/104026/

The drivetrain and pedestrian-warning loops are original
synthesized audio. The Model 3 forward and reverse warning tones were
matched by frequency analysis of Tesla's Normal Operating Sounds examples.
https://service.tesla.com/docs/Public/diy/model3/en_us/GUID-AA58ED67-9C93-4EE6-8B19-9FDABE018787.html
