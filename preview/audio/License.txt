Tesla Model Y (2021) recordings

Leon Müller and Wolfgang Kropp, Chalmers University of Technology (2024)
Auralization of Electric Vehicles for the Perceptual Evaluation of Acoustic Vehicle Alerting Systems
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10610491

Adapted from vehicleA_avas_forward_measured.wav (tesla_forward.wav),
vehicleA_avas_backward_measured.wav (tesla_reverse.wav), and
vehicleA_passBy_forwards_measured.wav (sedan_road.wav). Excerpts were filtered,
folded to mono where applicable, resampled, leveled, and crossfaded for looping.
